The Feelgood Company is a Bahrain-based hospitality and management consulting group. We create, operate and grow distinctive food, beverage and lifestyle concepts.

Our philosophy

The creativity of hospitality, with the discipline of business. Experiences that feel good for customers and make commercial sense for the business behind them.

Who we are

We bring together creative thinking, commercial understanding and hands-on operational expertise to help hospitality businesses go from first idea to sustainable operation.

Whether we are developing a new concept, improving an existing business or bringing a temporary activation to life, our approach remains practical, collaborative and focused on execution.

Our experience spans concept development, restaurant operations, food and beverage strategy, menu development, branding, marketing, team development and business advisory.

We believe that great hospitality is about more than good food or attractive spaces. It is about creating an experience that people enjoy, remember and want to return to.

  • Strategic advisory, investment and growth support

Our strength is bringing these disciplines together into one coordinated approach.

Our approach: the F&B business lifecycle

We see hospitality development as a continuous cycle rather than a one-time launch.

Every successful concept requires a clear idea, a sound business plan, the right people, disciplined execution and a commitment to continuous improvement.

1

Phase 1

Concept development

Every business begins with a clear proposition.

We help define the concept, customer experience, market positioning, food and beverage offering, brand direction and commercial opportunity.

This includes market research, competitor benchmarking, customer profiling, concept ideation and the development of a distinctive hospitality identity.

2

Phase 2

Business planning & design

Once the concept is established, we translate the vision into a practical business model.

This includes business planning, pricing strategy, operational requirements, menu development, kitchen planning, equipment recommendations, staffing structures and implementation planning.

The objective is to create a concept that is both appealing to customers and operationally achievable.

3

Phase 3

Team development & training

People are central to the hospitality experience.

We support recruitment, team structures, standard operating procedures and practical training across kitchen, service and bar operations.

Training covers product knowledge, recipes, service standards, operational routines, quality control and team coordination.

4

Phase 4

Implementation & launch

We help bring the concept to life.

Our involvement may include pre-opening coordination, menu testing, operational readiness, mock services, marketing campaigns, launch activations and hands-on support during opening.

We focus on ensuring that the experience promised by the concept can be delivered consistently in practice.

5

Phase 5

Post-implementation & optimisation

Opening is the beginning of the next stage.

We review performance, customer feedback, product quality, costs, service standards and operational efficiency to identify opportunities for improvement.

This may include menu engineering, pricing adjustments, staff development, marketing initiatives and operational restructuring.

The insights gained feed into the next cycle of business development and growth.

Our services

Eight ways we help, from the first concept to ongoing performance.

  • We develop hospitality concepts with a clear identity, relevant market positioning and a compelling customer experience.

    Services include concept creation, brand positioning, competitive analysis, customer journey development, food and beverage direction and creative strategy.

  • We create and refine menus that balance creativity, customer appeal, operational feasibility and profitability.

    Our work includes dish ideation, recipe development, ingredient sourcing, recipe costing, menu pricing, margin analysis, tastings, plating standards and menu optimisation.

    We also assess menu performance and identify opportunities to improve profitability, simplify execution and reduce waste.

  • We help build efficient kitchen operations that support the intended menu and service model.

    Services include kitchen workflow planning, equipment specifications, station design, procurement guidance, production systems, hygiene procedures, recipe documentation and back-of-house training.

  • We help create service experiences that are welcoming, efficient and consistent.

    This includes service procedures, guest experience standards, staffing coordination, team training, service simulations and ongoing quality assessment.

  • We support beverage concepts through menu development, cocktail creation, costing, presentation, operational setup and bar team training.

    Our focus is on delivering a beverage experience that complements the wider concept while remaining practical and commercially sound.

  • We help hospitality businesses build awareness, connect with their audiences and create reasons for customers to visit.

    Services include marketing strategy, content direction, social media planning, launch campaigns, event programming, collaborations, pop-ups and temporary hospitality activations.

  • We support the day-to-day and strategic requirements of hospitality operations.

    This includes operational reviews, staffing structures, procurement, cost control, performance management, service standards, quality assurance and continuous improvement.

  • We support business owners and investors in evaluating opportunities, planning expansion and developing sustainable growth strategies.

    Areas of focus include commercial planning, investment evaluation, partnership development, business development and strategic growth initiatives.

Our vision
